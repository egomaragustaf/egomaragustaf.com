## What transferred

Systems thinking. Naming things. The patience to read a stack trace. Most of being a decent engineer is not framework-specific, and that becomes obvious only in hindsight.

## What I had to unlearn

- Treating the UI as a thin view layer. State, layout, and animation are first-class concerns.
- Optimizing for the database first. Most pages are bounded by what the user can perceive, not by query cost.
- Avoiding re-renders as a moral virtue. Premature `useMemo` is real.

## What surprised me

How much joy there is in the small details — easing curves, focus rings, the exact frame a tooltip appears. I didn't expect to like it.
