## Tokens first

Before any component, settle on color, spacing, radius, and typography tokens. Everything else composes from them. If two components disagree, the tokens are wrong — not the components.

## Primitives over features

A `Button`, `Input`, and `Card` cover 80% of what you need. Resist building a `UserCard` or `ProductRow` in the library — those belong to the product, not the system.

## Delete more than you add

Every component is a maintenance liability. If something hasn't been used in three months, delete it. Git remembers.
